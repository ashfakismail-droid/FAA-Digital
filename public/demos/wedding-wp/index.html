Chapter one

With all our hearts

Beloved family and dearest friends — we are getting married. After letters, long train rides and one question asked in the blue of Santorini, we have chosen a day to begin our forever. We would be honoured to have you beside us in the gardens, at the table, and in every photograph we will keep for the rest of our lives.

Camille & Julian, with love, always

Chapter two

Our story

Four chapters, one rain-soaked afterthought of a coat,
and a box of letters still sitting by our bed.

2019

2019

The Atelier

Camille was restoring a 1904 botanical atlas when the rain came. Julian walked in, soaked to the collar, asking if they sold postcards. He left with her telephone number instead — and a small, smug smile she is still teasing him about.

2021

2021

The Letters

Two cities, one grey coat with a torn pocket, and stacks of letters Camille bound herself in cotton and wax. Julian's replies always ended the same way — “come home, I've saved you the good chair.” Every one of them is still in the box by our bed.

2023

2023

The Question

The ring lived for three days pressed between the pages of that same atlas, its corner suspiciously bulging. At golden hour above the caldera, Julian knelt and asked. Camille said yes before he finished the question.

2027

2027

The Day

We fell for a wisteria, a crumbling limonaia, and a gardener who swore he would keep our secrets. On the fifth of June the villa belongs to everyone we love — and our story becomes yours.

Chapter three

The weekend

Three days at the lake, beginning on the fourth of June.
If you can only make one, make the fifth — but we won't pretend it isn't nicer to make three.

  • Friday · 4 June

    18:30

    Aperitivo al Limone

    Lemon Garden, Villa Serenissima

    Venetian spritz, cold prosecco and little plates as the light amber and goes out. Summery garden attire — the lemons will be watching.

  • Saturday · 5 June

    16:00

    The Ceremony

    Wisteria Terrace, by the lake

    Under a canopy of wisteria. Kindly let phones rest in pockets — our photographer will keep the first kiss safe for you.

  • Saturday · 5 June

    18:30

    Dinner & Dancing

    The Limonaia Ballroom

    A candlelit dinner of ten tables, then dancing until the last ferry. Black tie optional; comfortable dancing shoes recommended.

  • Sunday · 6 June

    10:30

    The Morning After

    The Great Lawn

    Bloody Marys, cold prosecco, warm pastries and spilled stories. Come exactly as you danced.

Getting here

  • By airMilan Malpensa — 55 minutes by car or villa shuttle (booked at RSVP).
  • By railMilano Centrale → Como Lago (40 min), then the lake ferry to Lenno (35 min).
  • By waterPrivate boats from Como and Bellagio on request; the villa has its own pier.
  • By carParking is limited — please use the shuttle from Lenno car park, Saturday 12:30–15:30.

For your stay

We have reserved a room block at the Grand Hotel Victoria, Bellagio and smaller lake-house rooms at the Pensione Alpina, Lenno. Please mention Moretti–Hartwell before the first of April. The villa gardens open to guests from nine o'clock on Saturday morning.
